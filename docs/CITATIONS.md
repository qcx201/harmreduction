## Methodology Notes

### Point-in-Time (PiT) Counts
All official counts use the PiT methodology:
- Conducted over 24 hours or single night
- Combination of street counts and shelter surveys
- Volunteer-based enumeration
- Known to undercount actual homelessness by 15-30%

### Estimates
For years without official counts, estimates are based on:
1. Linear interpolation between known count years
2. Trend analysis from adjacent jurisdictions
3. Known events (e.g., pandemic impact, housing policy changes)
4. Research literature on homelessness trends

## Data Quality Ratings

- **official_count**: From actual PiT counts conducted by governments/organizations
- **research**: From peer-reviewed research or official research reports
- **estimate**: Interpolated or calculated based on trends and adjacent data
- **derived**: Calculated from other official statistics

## Limitations and Caveats

1. **Undercounting**: PiT counts typically miss 15-30% of homeless population
2. **Hidden Homelessness**: Couch-surfing and provisional arrangements not captured
3. **Methodological Variations**: Count methods varied between years and regions
4. **Data Gaps**: Not all years have official counts (typically every 2-3 years)
5. **COVID-19 Impact**: 2020-2022 data affected by pandemic disruptions to counting
